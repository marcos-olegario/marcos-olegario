### Hi, I'm Marcos! 👋

I'm a PhD student in Physics at the **São Carlos Institute of Physics, University of São Paulo (IFSC/USP)**, Brazil, working in **astroparticle physics**. My research focuses on **extensive air showers**: simulations and data analysis for **the Pierre Auger Observatory** and **Cherenkov Telescope Array Observatory (CTAO)**.

- 🔭 Direct Doctorate at IFSC/USP, funded by FAPESP and advised by Prof. Vitor de Souza
- 🌌 Research: air shower simulations and data analysis for CTAO
- 🪐 Background: B.Sc. in Computational Physics (USP), with an undergraduate research project (FAPESP) on dark matter models based on N-body simulations
- 🌠 Science communicator and guide at the Dietrich Schiel Observatory (CDCC/USP)
- 🤝 Open to research collaborations and to exchanging ideas on teaching and science outreach
